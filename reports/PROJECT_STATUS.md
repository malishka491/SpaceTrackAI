# SpaceTrack AI — Prototype Status

## Study area
Trishuli River Corridor, Nepal.

## Available outputs
- Sentinel-1 processed VV and VH bands.
- Preliminary low-backscatter screening raster.
- Road-level screening results.
- Streamlit dashboard with map layers and summary metrics.

## Current screening results
- Road features checked: 21,153
- Road features flagged by provisional threshold: 424
- Candidate pixels: 558,462
- Reported candidate share: 2.49%

## Limitations
The current screening is preliminary, not validated flood detection.
SNAP reported a possible calibration lookup-table issue.
A suitable comparable pre-event image and independent validation
are still required. Flagged roads are not confirmed damaged or unsafe.
